Privacy Policy

Effective 25 August 2026 · VideoSearch AI Chrome extension, Studio, and mobile apps

VideoSearch AI helps you search spoken words in YouTube captions, mark moments, and save frame shots. Search runs on your device. An account is optional and only used to sync notes you choose to keep.

What stays on your device

  • YouTube captions fetched for the video you are watching
  • On-device embeddings used for search (IndexedDB in Chrome)
  • Local marks, shots, and settings until you sign in
  • An optional AI API key, if you paste one in Settings — stored only in Chrome storage on that computer

Caption indexes and embeddings are not uploaded to VideoSearch servers.

What you can choose to sync

If you create an account, we store the email, name, and a session token you provide, plus the marks, screenshots, description/bio text, and source links you save. This vault is so Chrome, Studio, Android, and iPhone can show the same notes.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data
  • We do not use your captions or notes for advertising
  • We do not require an API key to search
  • We do not strip YouTube ads or replace the official player on the YouTube website

Permissions (Chrome)

  • storage — save settings, marks, shots, and the local caption index
  • clipboardWrite — copy a timestamp or source link when you ask
  • youtube.com — read captions and show the VideoSearch panel on watch pages
  • Model CDNs (Hugging Face, jsDelivr) — download the on-device search model once
  • Optional AI hosts — only if you set a Chat/Ask key in Settings
  • localhost / your vault host — optional account sync to your vault

Account deletion

Sign out in the extension to drop the session on that device. To delete vault data, sign in to Studio and remove videos, or contact us from the product site.
